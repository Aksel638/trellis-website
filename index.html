FAQ

Common questions

Why subscription instead of a one-time project fee?

A one-time build goes stale the day it launches. Your subscription covers hosting, security updates, and ongoing edits, so the site keeps working as your business changes — without a new invoice every time.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There's no long-term contract on any plan. If you cancel, we'll help you export your content and point your domain wherever you'd like.

What if I need more than my plan includes?

You can upgrade to the next plan at any time, or ask about a one-off addition — we'll quote it before doing any extra work.

Do you work with businesses outside restaurants?

Yes — Trellis works with most local, service-based and small retail businesses: salons, boutiques, contractors, studios, clinics and more.
